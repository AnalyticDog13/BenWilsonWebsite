Ben Wilson

Unit Still Photographer specializing in unit stills, BTS, and key art. Based in Los Angeles, member of the Unit Stills Directory.

Ben brings a cinematic sensibility to every set — whether capturing the quiet moments between takes or the electric energy of a difficult scene. His work has screened at Cannes, appeared in major trade publications, and contributed to the marketing campaigns of both independent films and studio productions.

Available in Los Angeles as a local, with regular availability in Cincinnati, Ohio and throughout Colorado.
